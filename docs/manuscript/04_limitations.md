# Limitations {#sec:limitations}

Daily activity and geophysical observations can both have temporal structure.
Calendar schedules, changes in project workload, sampling windows, and
other unmeasured variables can produce or obscure associations. A strong
lag found after scanning several shifts also reflects the search. Its
interpretation depends on the number of comparisons, temporal dependence,
and the suitability of the null model.

Commit counts represent recorded public activity on a platform. They cover
neither all work nor motivation. The archived author's example is useful
for inspecting the output format. It cannot support individual prediction
or generalization to people outside the observation.
